Final Fantasy IV (1991)

FFIV introduced the Active Time Battle system and delivered one of the first emotionally driven RPG narratives.

Final Fantasy V (1992)

Known for its deep Job System, FFV allowed unprecedented customization and replayability.

Final Fantasy VI (1994)

Often considered one of the greatest RPGs ever made, FFVI featured a massive cast and the unforgettable villain Kefka.
