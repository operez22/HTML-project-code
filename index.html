Brief Overview

Final Fantasy is a long-running series of Japanese role-playing games developed by Square Enix, Formerly known as Squaresoft. The series was created by programmer Hironobu Sakaguchi, who was working as a director at Square all the way back in 1987. Gamers in Japan were starting to have their eyes on Japanese Role-Playing Games, thanks to this and the already popular Dragon Quest developed by Enix. The late 1980s period would serve as the birth of the JRPG genre. It wouldnt take long for the series to become a massive success. In the 1990s, Square would frequently release titles that woukd become crittically acclaimed. Fans and critics began to love the franchise for its memorable characters, engaging storylines, and innovative gameplay mechanics. The series has since expanded to include numerous sequels, spin-offs, and adaptations across various media, solidifying its place as one of the most iconic and beloved franchises in the gaming industry.
